About the App
Eternus is a private memory system built on trust — not a social network. It lets you capture and preserve your memories across three layers: private (just for you), trusted circle (family and close friends), and legacy (long-term access you define and control). Optionally, a memory continuity feature allows your loved ones to stay connected to your story through the memories and recordings you chose to preserve.
Eternus will offer a free tier with core features available at no cost. Premium plans with expanded storage and advanced features will also be available. Full pricing details will be announced at launch.

Your Legacy
Inside the app, you assign people to your trust circles — family, close friends, or designated legacy contacts. Each person only sees what you have explicitly allowed them to see. You can change or revoke access at any time.
Yes. Any designated trusted contact can request deactivation of memory continuity at any time. We respect the emotional wellbeing of everyone who uses Eternus and understand that this feature may not be right for everyone.
If no legacy instructions are set and your account remains inactive for an extended period, your data will be securely retained for up to 10 years, after which it will be permanently deleted. We strongly encourage all users to configure their legacy settings to ensure their wishes are respected.
