About the App
Eternus is a private memory system built on trust — not a social network. It allows you to capture and preserve your memories across three layers: private (just for you), trusted circle (family and close friends), and legacy (controlled access after you're gone). Optionally, an AI presence allows your loved ones to feel connected to you, even after you pass.
Eternus will offer a free tier with core features available at no cost. Premium plans with expanded storage and advanced AI features will also be available. Full pricing details will be announced at launch.

AI Presence
Eternus uses artificial intelligence to learn from the memories, messages and media you share. Over time, it builds a model of your personality, tone and way of expressing yourself. Only the trusted contacts you personally designate can interact with your AI presence — never the general public.

Privacy & Security
Eternus works in three layers — private (only you), trusted circle (people you designate), and legacy (controlled future access). You decide what goes in each layer. No one else, including Eternus staff, can access your memories without your explicit permission.
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols. Our servers are hosted within the European Union in full compliance with GDPR. We take the security of your memories extremely seriously. For full details, please read our Privacy Policy.
If you delete your account, all your personal data and memories will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers within 30 days. We do not retain any copies after this period.

Your Legacy
Inside the app, you assign people to your trust circles — family, close friends, or designated legacy contacts. Each person only sees what you have explicitly allowed them to see. You can change or revoke access at any time.
Yes. Any designated trusted contact can request deactivation of the AI presence at any time. We respect the emotional wellbeing of those who interact with Eternus and understand that this feature may not be right for everyone.
If no legacy instructions are set and your account remains inactive for an extended period, your data will be securely retained for up to 10 years, after which it will be permanently deleted. We strongly encourage all users to configure their legacy settings to ensure their wishes are respected.
