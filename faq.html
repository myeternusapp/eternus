About Eternus

The essentials.

Eternus is a private space for preserving personal memories, meaningful relationships and the legacy a person chooses to build over time.

It combines Moments, guided reflection, intentional sharing and My Tree inside one experience designed around trust and control.

Eternus is not a public social network, memorial platform or digital simulation of a person.

No. Eternus does not use a public follower system or a public feed designed for reach, popularity or performance.

Private interactions such as likes and comments may exist inside trusted Circle and Legacy relationships.

No. Eternus is being built without advertising.

Memories & Moments

Capturing what matters.

You can preserve meaningful Moments using photographs, written stories, dates, places and personal context.

Additional media capabilities may evolve as Eternus develops.

Yes. You remain in control of the Moments you create and can update or remove them through the available controls in the app.

That depends on the layer you choose: Private, Circle or Legacy.

Nothing should be shared outside the layer and people you intentionally select.

Private, Circle & Legacy

How the three layers work.

Private is your personal space. Moments kept there are visible only to you.

Circle is for selected Moments you choose to share with people you trust today, such as family, close friends or meaningful connections.

Legacy is for memories, stories and access you intentionally preserve for the future and for the people you choose.

Yes. A person with Legacy access can also receive the Circle access shared with them.

Circle access alone does not include Legacy access.

Yes. Access is intentional and should remain under your control. You can update or revoke access through the available people-management controls.

Talk to Eternus

Guided reflection, not a chatbot.

Talk to Eternus is a guided reflection experience designed to help memories and meaningful stories emerge naturally.

It can explore people, places, experiences, work, family and personal moments worth preserving.

No. It is designed specifically around reflection, memory and personal storytelling rather than open-ended AI conversation.

No. Eternus does not claim to be a real person, recreate human consciousness or simulate a deceased person.

Its role is to help preserve and explore memories with emotional care.

Voice is an important part of the Eternus experience and is intended to make reflection feel more natural where supported.
