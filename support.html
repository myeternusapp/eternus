Bug reports

Details help us investigate faster.

When reporting an issue, include as much relevant information as you comfortably can.

  • What happened
  • What you expected to happen
  • The steps that caused the issue
  • Your device model
  • Your operating-system version
  • Your Eternus app version
  • A screenshot, if appropriate

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment details or highly sensitive personal information.

Before sending a screenshot, check whether it contains private names, photographs, messages or account details.

Private beta

Testing helps us improve the complete experience.

During private beta, reports from invited testers are especially valuable for identifying usability, reliability, privacy and device-specific issues.

When reporting a beta issue, mention whether it affects one account, one device or more than one tester.
