Built for today. Ready for tomorrow.

Technology will change. The way we preserve and experience our stories will evolve with it.

Eternus is built as a living platform — continuously evolving to embrace new possibilities while keeping your memories, relationships and legacy at its core.

What you preserve today will not be limited by today’s technology. It will become the foundation for richer and more meaningful ways to experience your story in the years ahead.

No expensive hardware. No inaccessible technology. Just a platform designed to evolve with you, your family and the future.

How Eternus is organised

Share with intention.

Every memory you keep in Eternus lives in one of three layers. You decide what belongs where — and you can change your mind at any time.

Private

Your personal space. Moments that remain entirely yours, visible only to you.

Tap to learn more

Private

Your personal space inside Eternus.

Memories saved here are visible only to you.

Use it for personal moments, thoughts, drafts, photographs, stories or anything you do not want to share.

Nothing moves to Circle or Legacy unless you intentionally change its visibility.

Tap to return

Circle

Share selected moments with the people closest to you, on your terms.

Tap to learn more

Circle

Share selected memories with the people you trust today.

You decide who belongs in your Circle and which moments they can see.

Circle is designed for family, close friends and meaningful connections.

Circle access does not automatically include Legacy access.

Tap to return

Legacy

Preserve what matters for the people and generations you choose. Legacy includes your Circle — Circle alone does not include Legacy.

Tap to learn more

Legacy

Preserve memories, stories, values and messages for the people you choose.

Legacy is designed for the future and for the people you intentionally include.

A person with Legacy access can also see Circle content shared with them.

Circle access alone does not include Legacy access.
