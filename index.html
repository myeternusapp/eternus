What Eternus offers

Not a social network.
A system of trust.

Memory Capture

A photo, a video, a voice note — captured in seconds. Every moment preserved exactly as you lived it, in your own words and images.

Private & Secure

Your memories belong to you. Every file is protected and only accessible to people you personally choose to trust.

Trust Circles

Three layers of access — private, trusted circle, and legacy. You decide exactly who sees what and when, with complete control always in your hands.

Controlled Access

No feed, no audience, no performance. Eternus is not social media — it is a private system of trust where permissions, not likes, define what is shared.

Memory Recovery

"What did my father say about this?" Eternus returns your memories with context, emotion and meaning — not just files, but lived moments.

AI Presence

Eternus learns your personality, your voice and your way of being — so that those you love can feel your presence, long after you are gone.
