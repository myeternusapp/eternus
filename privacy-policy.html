01

Who we are

Eternus is a private memory and legacy platform that helps people preserve personal Moments, reflect on meaningful experiences and share selected content with people they intentionally choose.

For privacy-related questions, contact: privacy@myeternusapp.com

02

Information we may process

Account information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication identifiers
  • Profile information
  • Account preferences

Content you choose to add

  • Photographs
  • Written stories and captions
  • Dates and places
  • Voice or video content where supported
  • Visibility and sharing choices
  • Comments, reactions and connection information

Technical and usage information

  • Device and operating-system information
  • App version
  • Authentication and session information
  • Timestamps
  • Diagnostic, performance and security information
  • Feature interactions where collected

The exact information processed depends on the features you use and the permissions you provide.

03

How we use information

  • To create and manage accounts
  • To store and display user-created Moments
  • To apply Private, Circle and Legacy access choices
  • To manage My Tree, invitations and connections
  • To provide Talk to Eternus and related reflection features
  • To maintain security, reliability and abuse prevention
  • To provide support and essential service communications
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations
  • To improve the product where permitted and appropriate

The applicable legal basis may include performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent or compliance with legal obligations, depending on the processing activity and applicable law.

04

Sharing and access choices

Eternus uses three primary access layers:

Private — content visible only to the account owner.

Circle — selected content shared with people chosen for present-day access.

Legacy — content and access intentionally preserved for the future.

Legacy access may include Circle access shared with that person. Circle access alone does not include Legacy access.

Users can manage or revoke access through the controls available in the app.

05

Talk to Eternus and AI

Talk to Eternus uses guided AI-assisted reflection to help users explore memories, people, places and experiences.

Eternus does not claim to recreate human consciousness, replace a real person or simulate a deceased person.

Depending on the implementation, prompts and related context may be processed by service providers required to deliver the feature.

AI-related data handling, retention and provider details must reflect the actual production configuration before public launch.

06

Service providers

Eternus relies on trusted service providers to operate parts of the service, including authentication, cloud storage, databases, hosting, communications and AI-related functionality where enabled.

These providers process information under their own contractual, security and privacy obligations.

The list of providers and processing locations may change as the product develops.

07

Data locations and international transfers

Data may be processed in more than one country, depending on the services and infrastructure used.

Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, Eternus will rely on applicable legal safeguards where required.

08

Security

Eternus uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration.

These measures may include authentication, access controls, secure transmission, platform security rules and restricted administrative access.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

09

Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, fulfil the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, maintain security and comply with legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary by data category, account status, feature and legal requirement.

Users may request account deletion through the available app controls or by contacting support.

10

Account deletion

When an account-deletion request is completed, associated personal information will be deleted or anonymised in accordance with the current technical process, applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

Some limited information may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution or backup-cycle completion.

11

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights including:

  • Access
  • Rectification
  • Erasure
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Objection
  • Withdrawal of consent
  • Complaint to a competent supervisory authority

To make a request, contact privacy@myeternusapp.com.

Eternus may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Legal rights may be subject to applicable conditions and exceptions. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

12

Children's privacy

Eternus is not currently intended for children under 18.

If we learn that personal information has been provided by a child in circumstances where parental or guardian consent is legally required and has not been obtained, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

13

Cookies and website technologies

The Eternus website may use technologies necessary for security, functionality and service operation.

Any analytics, preference or non-essential technologies should be described accurately and, where legally required, used only after appropriate consent.

14

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Eternus develops or as legal and operational requirements change.

The updated date will appear at the top of the page. Where appropriate, users may also be notified through the app, website or email.
