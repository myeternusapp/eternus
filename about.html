Our Story

Not a network.
A system of trust.

Eternus began with a quieter question: how do we preserve the moments, voices and stories that shape a life?

The Idea

Most of us have voice notes we've never listened back to, family photos spread across three different devices, and stories buried in old conversations. Moments that felt ordinary when they happened — and irreplaceable later.

Eternus was created to give those moments a home. To preserve the connection people leave behind through memories, voice and shared moments — built from the most personal thing any of us has: our stories.

Sometimes all we want is one more voice note. Eternus is not a social network. There are no likes, no feeds, no public performance. It is something far more human — a private system of memory and trust, designed for the people who matter most to you.

We believe that a life fully lived deserves to be preserved — and shared only with those who truly deserve to hold it.

"We are not building a social network. We are building infrastructure for human memory — with permission, with trust, and with love."

Our Mission

Our mission is to give every person the tools to preserve who they are — in their own words, their own images and their own voice — and to pass that on to the people who matter most.

Eternus works in three layers: what stays private, what is shared with your trusted circle, and what becomes your legacy. You remain in full control of every layer, at every stage.

We are building something closer to infrastructure than an app — a place where memory becomes legacy, where trust is the foundation, and where you are always in control.

Eternus is not about death. It is about life — and the quiet mark that every person leaves on those around them.

Our Values
01

Human First

02

Your memories are sacred. No feeds, no likes, no public exposure. We will never monetise, sell or compromise the personal data entrusted to us. Ever.

03

Trust by Design

Three layers — private, trusted circle, legacy. You decide what goes where, who has access, and when. Always in your hands. Always your choice.

04

We approach grief, loss and memory with the deepest respect. Eternus will never trivialise what it means to lose someone you love.
