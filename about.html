Our story

Not a network.
A system of trust.

Eternus began with a quieter question: how do we preserve the moments, voices and stories that shape a life — while keeping people in control of who can see them?

The idea

Some moments deserve more than storage.

Most of us have voice notes we rarely revisit, family photographs spread across different devices, and meaningful stories buried in old conversations.

Moments can feel ordinary when they happen and become deeply important later.

Eternus was created to give those moments context, connection and a place designed around trust.

Why Eternus exists

Built for life as it is lived.

Eternus helps people preserve memories in their own words, images and voice — and share them only with the people they intentionally choose.

It is designed for everyday moments, family stories, personal reflections, meaningful relationships and the legacy a person chooses to build over time.

"Your memories remain yours. Sharing is always intentional."

How Eternus is organised

Three layers. One clear choice: yours.

Your personal space for memories that remain entirely yours.

Selected moments shared with the people you trust today.

Memories, stories and access intentionally preserved for the future.

Legacy includes Circle access. Circle access alone does not include Legacy.

More than storage

Eternus gives memories meaning through context.

Preserve photographs, videos, stories, dates and places together.

Use guided reflection to explore experiences and uncover stories worth preserving.

Invite family, close friends and meaningful connections with intentional access.

Choose what matters, who becomes part of it and what they can access.

What makes Eternus different

Private by design.
Human by nature.

  • No public follower system
  • No public performance or popularity metrics
  • No advertising
  • No automatic sharing

There is no public feed designed for attention, reach or performance. Private interactions such as likes and comments may exist inside trusted Circle and Legacy relationships.

Our values

What guides how we build.

Technology should support real relationships, not replace them.

Access begins private and changes only through clear, intentional choices.

You decide what is preserved, where it belongs and who can access it.

Eternus treats personal memories, relationships and life stories with care and respect.

Eternus is being built without advertising and around strict access boundaries.

Private beta

Currently in private beta.

We are refining Eternus with a small group of invited testers before a wider release.

The goal is not to launch quickly. It is to build something people can trust.
