# What is the Legacy Profile?

Your **Legacy Profile** preserves more context about your life, alongside your Moments. It can include your date and place of birth, current location, nationality, languages, profession, places you've lived, an **"About me"** section and a **message for future generations**.

You edit the Legacy Profile in the Eternus app. On Eternus Web, Edit Profile covers your name, bio, date of birth, place of birth, current location, photo and banner, but not the other Legacy Profile details.

The message for future generations is kept as part of your Legacy Profile. It isn't scheduled and isn't sent to anyone automatically.

Your bio and your "About me" are separate fields.
