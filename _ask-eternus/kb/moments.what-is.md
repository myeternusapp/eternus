# What is a Moment?

A **Moment** is a memory you intentionally preserve in Eternus. It can include a title and a written story, a date or a period, a location, and media such as photos, video or a voice recording.

**Moments are Private by default.** Creating a Moment doesn't share it with anyone. You decide whether it also goes to your Circle, your Legacy or both.

A Moment belongs to the person who created it. Sharing it lets selected people see it; it doesn't make them owners, and they can't edit or delete it.

What you can add depends on where you create the Moment — see *How do I create a Moment?* and *Photos, video and voice in Moments*.
