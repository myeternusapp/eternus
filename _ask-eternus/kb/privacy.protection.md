# How does Eternus protect my information?

Eternus is private by default, and access depends on explicit choices:

- **Access is checked by Eternus**, based on the owner's current choices — not only by what a screen shows. Having a link to a photo or video doesn't by itself give access to it.
- **Media follows its Moment:** if you can't see a Moment, you can't see its photos, video or audio.
- **Removing access stops it from then on.** Eternus can't remove copies someone made outside Eternus while they had access, such as screenshots, photos of a screen or downloads.
- **On Android**, certain screens can be protected against screenshots. This isn't confirmed on other platforms, and it can't stop someone photographing a screen.
- **Sharing never requires your password.** People get access through invitations they accept, not by using your account.

No system can promise absolute protection, so Eternus describes the protections it actually provides. For formal details, read the Eternus Privacy Policy on myeternusapp.com, or write to privacy@myeternusapp.com.
