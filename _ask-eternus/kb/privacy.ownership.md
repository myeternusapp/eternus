# Who owns my memories?

**Your memories remain yours.** The Moments, profile and content you create belong to you.

- Sharing a Moment with Circle or Legacy lets selected people see it. It doesn't make them owners, and they can't edit or delete it.
- Each person keeps control of their own profile, Moments and access choices — including the people in your Digital Tree.
- Being connected to someone — in the Digital Tree, through a relationship or through an invitation — doesn't by itself give access to their content.
- Eternus doesn't share something just because it was created, and never moves a Moment to another layer on its own.

You decide what to preserve, what to share and with whom, and you can change or remove access later.
