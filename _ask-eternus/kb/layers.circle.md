# What is Circle?

**Circle** is for trusted sharing in the present — with people such as family, close friends or other meaningful connections.

People get Circle access when they accept a Circle invitation from you. They can then see the Moments you've shared with Circle.

They can't see your Private Moments, and **Circle access alone doesn't include your Legacy Moments**.

Circle isn't a followers list or an audience, and sharing with Circle never makes a Moment public.
