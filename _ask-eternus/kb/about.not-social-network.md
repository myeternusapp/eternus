# What Eternus is not

**Eternus is not a social network.** It isn't built around followers, likes, popularity, public feeds or engagement metrics. Sharing is intentional and personal. Hearts and comments exist only on Moments that have already been shared with you through Circle or Legacy.

Eternus is also not:

- **a public memorial platform** — nothing in Eternus is published as a public page;
- **cloud storage with a different interface** — it preserves the story and context around photos and other media, not just files;
- **a genealogy service** — the Digital Tree gives human context to the people in your life; it isn't for researching ancestry;
- **an AI clone of a person, or digital immortality** — Eternus doesn't recreate anyone or speak as anyone.

Eternus doesn't decide what deserves to be remembered. That decision belongs to you.
