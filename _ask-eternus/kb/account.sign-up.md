# How do I create an Eternus account?

You can create an Eternus account in the Eternus app or on Eternus Web. You provide your **name**, **email address** and a **password**. Some additional personal details may be requested while your profile is set up.

After you sign up, Eternus sends you a **verification email**. You need to verify your email address before you can use Eternus (see *How do I verify my email?*).

If you already have an Eternus account, use **Sign In** instead of creating a second account.

Creating an account doesn't add anyone to your Circle or Legacy, doesn't accept any invitation by itself and doesn't share anything. Your space starts Private.
