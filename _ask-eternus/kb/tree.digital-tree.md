# What is the Digital Tree (My Tree)?

The **Digital Tree** — shown as **My Tree** — is a visual way to see and move between the meaningful people in your Eternus. Today, your tree is built around your established Legacy relationships.

From the tree you can open a person to see their profile and, where they have given you access, the content they share with you. You appear in your own tree as **YOU**; your own details are in My Profile.

Being in someone's tree, or being related to them, doesn't give access to their Moments. Access comes only from an accepted invitation. Moving through the tree doesn't give you access to the other people you see there either.

The Digital Tree isn't a genealogy tool. You can't freely build a family tree, add any person you like or research ancestry.

On Eternus Web, **My Tree** lets you view your tree and open people's Profile and Legacy. Inviting people and managing access are done in the Eternus app.
