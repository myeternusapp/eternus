# What can I do on Eternus Web?

**Eternus Web** (app.myeternusapp.com) works in a web browser. On it you can:

- create an account, verify your email and sign in;
- view and edit your profile (**My Profile → Edit Profile**);
- view your own Moments by layer and create new Moments with text and photos (**My Moments**);
- explore your Digital Tree and open the Profile and Legacy of people who've given you access (**My Tree**).

**Not currently available on Eternus Web:**

- editing or deleting Moments;
- adding video or voice;
- Current Moment;
- inviting people, accepting invitations and managing access;
- adding or tagging people in Moments;
- hearts, comments and the Inbox;
- Talk;
- Memorials;
- the Legacy Profile details beyond Edit Profile, most Settings, data export and starting account deletion.

Eternus Web itself explains: *"On Eternus Web you can view and edit your profile details, explore your Digital Tree and create Moments. For features not yet available on Web, use the Eternus app."*

Your account, Moments and access are the same on the web and in the app. Using the web never gives anyone more access than the app.
