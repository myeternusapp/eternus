# How do I create a Moment?

**In the Eternus app** you can create a Moment with a title, story, date or period, location, and photos, video or voice, and choose its layers.

**On Eternus Web:**

1. Open **My Moments** and select **Add a Moment**.
2. Add a title, a story, a single date or a period, a location (free text, with place suggestions where available) and, if you like, photos.
3. Choose who can see it: **Private is always included**; you can add Circle, Legacy or both.
4. Select **Save Moment**.

A Moment needs at least a title, a story or one photo. Nothing is uploaded or saved until you select Save Moment. If you started from the Circle or Legacy view, that layer is already selected.

**Cancel** closes an empty editor straight away. If you've started a draft, Eternus asks whether to discard it; a discarded draft isn't saved.

If a photo can't be uploaded or the Moment can't be saved, the editor keeps your draft so you can try again. A Moment is never partly saved.

Moments created on the web are the same as Moments created in the app and follow the same limits. You can view them in both, and edit or delete them in the app. Video, voice and adding people to a Moment aren't available on Eternus Web.
