# How do I delete a message in Messenger?

**Messenger is being prepared for the Beta of the Eternus Android app. It may not be available in your version of the app yet.**

To delete a message, press and hold it, then choose an option:

- **Delete for me** removes the message only from your own conversation. The other person's copy stays as it is. You can do this at any time, for messages you sent or received.
- **Delete for both** removes the message text from both conversations. Only the person who sent the message can choose it, and only during the first 24 hours after sending. After 24 hours, the sender can only use **Delete for me**.

If **Delete for both** is no longer possible, you can still choose **Delete for me**.

A deleted message leaves a small **Message deleted** note in the conversation, which still shows who sent it and when.

**What deleting can't do:** it can't remove screenshots or other copies someone has made, or undo the fact that the other person may already have read the message. If a device is offline, the change appears when it reconnects.

You can still delete messages after blocking someone or after access between you has been removed.
