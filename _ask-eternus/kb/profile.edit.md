# How do I edit my profile?

**In the Eternus app** you can edit your profile information, photo, banner and bio. Place of birth and the other Legacy Profile details are edited in the Legacy Profile (see *What is the Legacy Profile?*).

**On Eternus Web**, open **My Profile** and select **Edit Profile**. You can change:

- **Full name** (required)
- **Bio**
- **Date of birth** — pick a date, or remove it
- **Place of birth** and **Current location** — free text, with place suggestions where available
- **Profile photo** and **banner**

Select **Save** to keep your changes; My Profile shows them straight away. **Cancel** leaves without saving. If you've made changes, Eternus asks you to confirm first.

If your date of birth was saved in an older format, it's shown as saved, with a note. It stays as it is unless you choose a new date or remove it.

Photos on the web: a new profile photo is resized to fit within 400 × 400 pixels and a banner within 1200 × 480 pixels. They keep their proportions and aren't cropped or enlarged. Nothing is uploaded until you select Save. If saving fails, the editor stays open with what you entered so you can try again.

Edit Profile is available only on your own profile. Editing your profile doesn't change who can see it or your Moments.
