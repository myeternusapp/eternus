# Signing in, resetting your password and signing out

**Sign in** with the email address and password of your Eternus account, in the Eternus app or on Eternus Web.

**Forgot your password?** You can request a password reset by email.

**Signing out** ends your session on that device or browser. It doesn't delete your account, your Moments, your profile or your relationships, and you can sign in again later.

When you sign in again, Eternus uses your current access. If someone removed your access while you were signed out, it doesn't come back because you signed in.

Never share your password with anyone. **Eternus support will never ask for your password.**
