# What is Talk?

**Talk** is a chat-style experience in the Eternus app. You can type, or use your device's voice dictation to turn speech into text. Talk keeps your conversation history and lets you start a new conversation.

**What Talk is today:** a limited, basic experience. Its replies come from a set of predefined responses chosen by keywords, so a reply can be general, or may not match what you meant.

**What Talk doesn't do:**

- It doesn't read or look through your Moments, and doesn't use them to shape its replies.
- It doesn't learn about you from what you preserve.
- It doesn't speak as you or as any other person, and it doesn't recreate anyone — it isn't an AI clone.
- It can't change your account, your access or your Moments.

Talk isn't available on Eternus Web.

**Looking ahead:** Eternus is exploring a future version of Talk with AI. The direction is that it could use information you're allowed to see in Eternus, including Moments, while respecting everyone's access and privacy choices. Private content would never become available to another person through AI. This is an exploratory direction, not an available or scheduled feature, and no release date has been set.

Eternus doesn't intend to digitally resurrect, recreate or impersonate anyone. A future version might let Eternus AI be heard in an authorised loved one's voice, but it would still be Eternus AI speaking, never that person. How such a voice would be authorised hasn't been decided.
