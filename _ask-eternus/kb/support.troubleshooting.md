# Common problems and what to try

**The verification email hasn't arrived.** Check your spam or junk folder, then request a new verification email from the verification screen and use the newest link.

**The verification page says the link may be invalid or may already have been used.** Request a new verification email and use the newest link. If you've already verified, go back to Eternus and select "I've verified my email".

**The verification screen doesn't continue after I verified.** Return to the app or to the Eternus Web tab and give it a moment, or select "I've verified my email".

**I can't sign in.** Check your email address and password, or request a password reset email. Eternus support will never ask for your password.

**A photo won't add on Eternus Web.** If a file can't be read as a photo, Eternus tells you and leaves it out — try a different photo. If an upload fails, check your connection and select Save again; your draft is kept.

**I can't find a feature on Eternus Web.** Some features are only in the app (see *What can I do on Eternus Web?*).

**I can't see someone's Moments.** You can see only the Moments the owner has shared with a layer you've accepted access to — Circle or Legacy. Being in the same Digital Tree isn't enough, and only the owner can change it.

For anything else, contact support@myeternusapp.com with the details listed in *How do I contact Eternus support?*
