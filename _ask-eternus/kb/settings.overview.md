# What's in Settings?

Settings in the Eternus app include:

- **account security** — email, password, biometric unlocking and sessions (see *Account security: email, password, biometrics and sessions*);
- **appearance** — text size and interface transitions;
- **which layer Home opens on**;
- **local reminders**, such as weekly or monthly reminders and recaps. They are notifications created on your device with set messages; they aren't summaries of your Moments;
- **language** — English, Portuguese, Italian, Greek and Spanish can be chosen, but much of Eternus is still in English and some languages may show as not yet available;
- **Clear Cache**, which clears data the app keeps on your device;
- **data export** and **account deletion**.

Some options in Settings — for example a dark theme or extra colour options — are marked as not yet available, and some stored preferences don't yet change how Eternus works. An option being visible doesn't mean the feature is complete.

Most settings aren't available on Eternus Web.
