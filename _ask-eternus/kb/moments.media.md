# Photos, video and voice in Moments

**Photos** — you can add several photos to a Moment, in the app and on Eternus Web. Photos can be opened larger.

**Video (app only)** — choose a video from your device or record one with the camera. Eternus tries to create a thumbnail for it.

**Voice (app only)** — record a voice recording of up to five minutes while preparing a Moment, and listen to it before saving. Recordings aren't transcribed automatically.

**Photos on Eternus Web** are prepared in your browser as JPEG images and shown as a preview before saving. They're scaled down so that their longer side is at most 1200 pixels, keeping their proportions, without cropping or enlarging. They're uploaded only when you select Save Moment. A file that can't be read as a photo is left out, with a message.

Eternus Web can show photos and play video and audio from Moments you can access.

Media follows the Moment it belongs to: only people who can see the Moment can see its photos, video and audio.
