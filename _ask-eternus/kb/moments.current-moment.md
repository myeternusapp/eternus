# What is Current Moment?

**Current Moment** is a simple way to show what's happening in your life right now. You create it in the Eternus app with a photo and a short text.

- It appears on your Home, and you can change or delete it whenever you like.
- It doesn't expire on its own.
- When you replace it, Eternus can offer to keep the previous one as a separate Private Moment in your archive.
- Other people can see your Current Moment only if their access allows it, for example through Circle or Legacy access. Seeing it doesn't let them change it.

Current Moment isn't available on Eternus Web.
