# Is the Eternus mobile app in Beta?

Yes. **The Eternus mobile app is currently in Beta.** It is still being verified, refined and corrected, and parts of it may change.

While using the Beta you may see unfinished areas, disabled options or **Coming Soon** labels. These don't mean the feature is available, and they aren't a promise that it will launch, or launch in that form.

The public Eternus website (myeternusapp.com) is in production; it is not a Beta.

If something doesn't work as expected, or you have feedback, contact Eternus support (see *How do I contact Eternus support?*). Feedback is reviewed carefully, but a suggestion isn't a commitment to add a feature.
