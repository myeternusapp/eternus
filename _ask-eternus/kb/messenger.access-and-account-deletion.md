# What happens to Messenger conversations if access is removed or an account is deleted?

**Messenger is being prepared for the Beta of the Eternus Android app. It may not be available in your version of the app yet.**

**If access is removed:** when neither of you has Circle or Legacy access from the other any more, you can't send each other new messages, but your conversation history stays. If some access remains — another layer, or access given in the other direction — you can still message each other, unless one of you has blocked the other.

**If you delete your Eternus account:** your own copies of your conversations are removed. The people you messaged keep the messages they received from you in their conversations in Eternus, including the text and the fact that you sent them. A **Deleted account** label may appear, but it doesn't make those messages anonymous. They can delete their own copies.

To remove a message from the other person's conversation as well, use **Delete for both** within 24 hours of sending it (see *How do I delete a message in Messenger?*). Deleting your account, removing someone's access or blocking someone doesn't erase the other person's conversation.
