# What is Legacy?

**Legacy** is for what you intentionally choose to preserve for the people you've entrusted with Legacy access.

How Legacy works today:

- People get Legacy access when they accept a Legacy invitation from you. **Their access starts as soon as they accept.**
- They can then see your Legacy Moments **and** your Circle Moments.
- They can't see your Private Moments.
- Circle access alone doesn't include Legacy.

Legacy access doesn't make anyone an owner of your Moments, and it doesn't let them edit or delete them. You can change or remove someone's Legacy access (see *Changing or removing someone's access*).
