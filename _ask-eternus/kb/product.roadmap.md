# Can I see Eternus's roadmap?

No. Eternus doesn't currently publish a public product roadmap, and its internal roadmap isn't publicly accessible.

When appropriate, Eternus may share news about planned or exploratory features through its official channels. Planned or exploratory features aren't available yet, and a roadmap target isn't a commitment to a release date.

You're welcome to send suggestions to support@myeternusapp.com; they're reviewed as product feedback.
