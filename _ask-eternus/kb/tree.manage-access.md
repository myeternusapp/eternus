# Changing or removing someone's access

You can change or remove the access you've given someone, from the **Eternus app**.

- **Remove one layer:** if someone has both Circle and Legacy access, you can, for example, remove Legacy and keep Circle.
- **Remove all access:** they can no longer see your Circle or Legacy Moments.
- The change applies to what they can see from then on, whether or not they've seen the content before.

Removing access doesn't delete your Moments — they stay in your archive with their current layers — and it doesn't transfer anything to the other person. It also can't remove copies they made outside Eternus while they had access, such as screenshots or downloads.

To give access again later, send a new invitation. Access you removed doesn't come back on its own.

Managing access isn't currently available on Eternus Web.
