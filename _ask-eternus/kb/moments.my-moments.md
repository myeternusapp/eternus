# Viewing your Moments: Home and My Moments

Your own Moments appear in a timeline: on **Home** in the app, and in **My Moments** on Eternus Web. You can view them one layer at a time:

- **Private** shows all of your own Moments;
- **Circle** shows the Moments you've shared with Circle;
- **Legacy** shows the Moments you've shared with Legacy.

A Moment shared with both Circle and Legacy appears under both. Choosing a view doesn't change who can see anything.

Your timeline can also include Moments created by other people that you've accepted into your timeline. They still belong to the people who created them.

The timeline is ordered mainly by when Moments were added to Eternus, not by the date of the memory itself.

Home isn't a social feed: it doesn't automatically collect everything shared by the people in your Circle.

In the app, you can choose in Settings which layer Home opens on. On Eternus Web, wide screens show the layers as a list on the left; on phones they appear above the timeline.
