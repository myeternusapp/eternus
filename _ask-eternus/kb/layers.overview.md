# Private, Circle and Legacy: who can see what?

Eternus has three layers that decide who can see a Moment:

- **Private** — only you.
- **Circle** — people you trust today, who have accepted Circle access from you.
- **Legacy** — what you intentionally preserve for the people you've entrusted with Legacy access, once they've accepted it.

Every Moment starts Private. You choose whether to also share it with Circle, Legacy or both. Eternus never moves a Moment to another layer on its own.

Who can see what:

- **Circle access** → your Circle Moments.
- **Legacy access** → your Legacy Moments and your Circle Moments.
- **Neither** includes your Private Moments.

Layers describe access and intent, not how important a memory is. A Private memory can matter just as much as a Legacy one.
