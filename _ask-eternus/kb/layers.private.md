# What is Private?

**Private** is your personal space and the starting point for every Moment. A Moment that is only Private can be seen by you alone.

Having Circle or Legacy access to you doesn't let anyone see your Private Moments — and neither does being in your Digital Tree or being related to you.

A Moment stays Private unless you choose to share it with Circle or Legacy.
