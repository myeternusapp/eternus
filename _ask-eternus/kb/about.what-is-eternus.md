# What is Eternus?

Eternus is a private digital space for preserving personal memories, relationships and family history — built for today and ready for tomorrow.

In Eternus you preserve **Moments**: memories with their story, dates, places and media. You can connect them to the people who matter to you through the **Digital Tree**.

Everything you create is **Private** by default. You decide whether a Moment is also shared with your **Circle** (people you trust today) or your **Legacy** (what you choose to preserve for the people you entrust with Legacy access).

Each person keeps control of their own profile, Moments and access choices. Being connected to someone never means unrestricted access to what they preserve.

Eternus is meant to be useful today — capturing a Moment, organising memories and sharing selected parts of life with trusted people — not only something to think about later.
