# How do I block or unblock someone in Messenger?

**Messenger is being prepared for the Beta of the Eternus Android app. It may not be available in your version of the app yet.**

In a conversation, open the conversation menu and choose **Block contact**. To undo it, choose **Unblock contact** in the same menu.

**What blocking does:**

- It stops new messages in both directions: while the block is in place, neither of you can send the other new messages.
- Your existing conversation stays, and you can still delete messages.
- It doesn't change Circle or Legacy access, your Digital Tree or anyone's Moments.

If you block someone before either of you has sent a message, an empty conversation stays in your list so that you can unblock them later.

**Unblocking** removes only your own block. You can send messages again only if the other person hasn't also blocked you and Circle or Legacy access between you still allows it.

Blocking is different from removing someone's access to your Moments (see *Changing or removing someone's access*).
