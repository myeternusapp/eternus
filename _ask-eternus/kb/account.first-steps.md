# What happens after I verify my email?

After your email is verified, Eternus checks that your account is ready. If needed, it guides you through a short account preparation and profile setup before you enter.

- **On Eternus Web**, you start in **My Profile**. From the header you can go to **My Moments** and **My Tree**.
- **In the Eternus app**, you get the broader Eternus experience, including Home with your Moments, creating Moments, the Digital Tree, the Inbox, your profile and Settings.

Your account starts with nothing shared. No one is added to your Circle or Legacy until you invite them and they accept.
