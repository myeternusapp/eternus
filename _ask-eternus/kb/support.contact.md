# How do I contact Eternus support?

- **Support — support@myeternusapp.com:** technical issues, bug reports, account or access issues, feature suggestions and general product questions.
- **Privacy — privacy@myeternusapp.com:** personal-data questions, privacy requests and concerns about how information is handled.
- **General — hello@myeternusapp.com:** general enquiries about Eternus.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include: what you were trying to do, what happened, what you expected, the steps that led to it, whether you were using the app or Eternus Web, your device model and operating-system version, your Eternus app version, and a screenshot if appropriate.

**Never send passwords, verification codes, payment details or highly sensitive personal information.** Before sending a screenshot, check that it doesn't show private names, photos, messages or account details you don't want to share.

Eternus support will never ask for your password.
