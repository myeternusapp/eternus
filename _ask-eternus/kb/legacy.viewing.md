# Viewing someone else's Legacy

When someone has given you Legacy access, you can view the Moments they share with you through Legacy, including their photos, video and audio.

**On Eternus Web:** open **My Tree**, select the person, and switch between their **Profile** and **Legacy**. **Back** returns you to the tree. In the app, you also reach connected people through the Digital Tree.

You stay yourself while viewing — you don't enter their account. Viewing their Legacy doesn't let you edit, delete or own their Moments, and it doesn't give you access to their Private Moments.

If they remove your access, you can no longer see that content.
