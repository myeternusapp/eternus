# Can I export my data?

In the Eternus app you can request an **export of your account data** as a JSON file (a structured data file).

The export contains selected account information. It isn't a complete copy of everything in your Eternus — it may not include all of your media or every kind of information — and it isn't a backup that can be used to restore your account.

Requesting an export doesn't change or delete anything in your account.

Data export isn't currently available on Eternus Web.
