# What is Messenger?

**Messenger is being prepared for the Beta of the Eternus Android app. It may not be available in your version of the app yet.**

Messenger is for private, one-to-one text conversations between people connected in Eternus. You start or open a conversation with **Chat** or **Message** on the profile of someone you can message.

**Who you can message:** to send new messages, your email address must be verified, and one of you must have given the other Circle or Legacy access that is still active. Messenger doesn't change anyone's access to Moments.

**Using Messenger:**

- Each message can be up to 2,000 characters of text.
- Longer conversations load in parts: at the top of a conversation, choose **Load earlier messages** to see older ones.
- You can send up to 20 new messages per minute in total, across all your conversations. If the app asks you to wait, pause for a moment before trying again.

**What Messenger doesn't include:** attachments such as photos, videos or files, push notifications, reactions to messages or read receipts. Messages aren't end-to-end encrypted.

Messenger is only in the Android app; it isn't available on Eternus Web. It's separate from **Talk**, from the **Inbox** and from Ask Eternus on the Eternus website.
