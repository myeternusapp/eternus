# What does My Profile show, and who can see my profile?

**My Profile** is your own profile in Eternus. It can show your profile photo and banner, your name and bio, your email, when you became a member, your Circle and Legacy counts, and the **About You** details: date of birth, place of birth and current location.

A date of birth is shown in a readable form, for example "March 12, 1990". An empty detail is shown as a dash.

The email and account details on My Profile are there for you. Other people in Eternus may see a reduced version of your profile — such as your name, photo, banner and bio — where you appear to them, while your other personal details stay under Eternus access controls. Your profile isn't a public web page.

My Profile shows who you are. Your Moments are on **Home** in the app and in **My Moments** on Eternus Web.

To change your details, see *How do I edit my profile?*
