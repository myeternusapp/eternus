# Features that aren't currently available

These aren't currently available in Eternus:

- **AI features that use your Moments** — for example answering questions about your archive or learning about you from what you preserve (see *What is Talk?*);
- **automatic transcription** of voice recordings;
- **two-factor authentication**;
- **signing out other devices remotely**;
- **a complete backup** of your account, or restoring a deleted account.

Some features are planned but not available yet, such as an iPhone (iOS) app (see *Which devices and platforms can I use?*) and adding video on Eternus Web (see *What can I do on Eternus Web?*).

If you see a feature in the app with a **Coming Soon** label, that isn't a promise that it will become available, or when. No individual feature has a promised release date.

Eternus's current roadmap target is to have its planned functionality available no later than the first half of 2027. This is a target, not a guarantee, and it isn't a release date for any individual feature.

You're welcome to send suggestions to support@myeternusapp.com; they're reviewed as product feedback.
