# Features that aren't currently available

These aren't currently available in Eternus:

- **AI features based on your Moments** — for example answering questions about your archive, learning about you from what you preserve, or recreating a person;
- **automatic transcription** of voice recordings;
- **two-factor authentication**;
- **signing out other devices remotely**;
- **a complete backup** of your account, or restoring a deleted account.

If you see one of these mentioned in the app, for example with a **Coming Soon** label, that isn't a promise that it will become available, or when.

Eternus doesn't share release dates for features. You're welcome to send suggestions to support@myeternusapp.com; they're reviewed as product feedback.
