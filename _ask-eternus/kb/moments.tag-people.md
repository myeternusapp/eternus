# How do I tag someone in a Moment?

In the **Eternus app** you can tag the people who were part of a Moment.

- **While adding a Moment:** use the optional **Tag people in this Moment…** field, or type **@** in the story and pick a name. The tags are added when you save the Moment.
- **For a Moment you've already saved:** open it in **View Moment** and select **Tag people**.

**Who you can tag:** people who have active Circle or Legacy access from you, when that access covers one of the Moment's layers. For example, to tag someone who has Circle access, the Moment needs to be shared with Circle. A Moment that is only Private can't be tagged, and you can't tag yourself. If none of the people you chose can be tagged, Eternus tells you that they may not have access to this Moment yet.

**What a tag does:** the tagged person receives the Moment in their Inbox and can accept or reject it. A tag never gives anyone access they didn't already have, and it doesn't change who can see the Moment. The Moment stays yours.

Tagging isn't available on Eternus Web.
