# How do I verify my email?

After you create your account, Eternus sends you a verification email.

1. Open the email and select the verification link. It opens an Eternus verification page in your browser.
2. The page confirms your email address. When it shows **"Your email has been verified"**, you can select **Continue** to open Eternus Web, or go back to the Eternus app or to the Eternus Web tab where you signed up.
3. When you return, the verification screen checks again on its own and continues once your email is verified.

If it doesn't continue, select **"I've verified my email"**. If your email isn't verified yet, the screen tells you and stays where it is.

If the verification page says the link may be invalid or may already have been used, request a new verification email from the verification screen and use the newest link.

Verifying your email confirms that the address is yours. It doesn't give anyone access to your content, and it doesn't give you access to anyone else's.
