# How do invitations work?

You invite someone from the **Eternus app**, using their email address. You choose whether the invitation is for **Circle** or **Legacy**, and you can describe your relationship — for example family (including a specific family relationship), friend, partner or other.

- **Sending an invitation doesn't give access yet.** The invitation stays pending until the person accepts it.
- The person receives an invitation email with a link to get started. If they don't have an Eternus account, they create one and verify their email. Creating the account doesn't accept the invitation by itself.
- The invitation is meant for the person it was sent to. Accepting it requires being signed in as that person, with a verified email.
- Pending invitations appear in the **Inbox** in the Eternus app, where they can be accepted or declined. Eternus Web doesn't currently include the Inbox.
- When an invitation is accepted, access starts straight away, for the layer the invitation was for. Declining gives no access.
- If someone already has one type of access, you can invite them separately to the other. Their existing access stays while the new invitation is pending.

In the app you can see the invitations you've sent, including pending ones.

The relationship you describe explains how you're connected. It doesn't decide what someone can see.
