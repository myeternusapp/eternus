# Account security: email, password, biometrics and sessions

In the Eternus app's Settings you can:

- **change your email address or password** — Eternus asks you to confirm your identity first;
- **turn on biometric unlocking** on supported devices — a local convenience for opening Eternus. It doesn't replace your password and isn't two-factor authentication;
- **view session information** for your account and **end the session on the current device**.

Signing out other active sessions remotely isn't currently available, and Eternus doesn't currently offer two-factor authentication.

On Android, Eternus can protect certain screens against screenshots. This protection is confirmed only on Android, and it can't stop someone photographing a screen.

These options are in the Eternus app. Eternus Web doesn't offer most settings.
